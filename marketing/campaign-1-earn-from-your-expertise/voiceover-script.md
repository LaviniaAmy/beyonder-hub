For `earn-from-your-expertise-v5.mp4` (45 seconds). About 85 words, read at a calm, warm pace (around 2 words a second) with room to breathe between scenes.

**Tone:** warm, confident and professional, like a colleague recommending something, not an advert shouting. Smile slightly while reading.
**Say "SEND" as the word "send"**, not as letters.

## Recording tips

- Record each paragraph as a **separate take** with a second of silence before and after. I'll place each one at its start time above, so you don't need to hit the timings exactly.
- Use a quiet room with soft furnishings (a wardrobe full of clothes is ideal). Hold the phone or mic about a hand's width from your mouth, slightly to one side.
- Send **WAV or high-quality MP3** files. Voice memos from an iPhone are fine.

## Music

- Calm, hopeful and light: soft piano, plucked strings or gentle ambient, around 80–100 BPM, with no vocals.
- Royalty-free sources: YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay Music, Uppbeat. Check that the licence allows use in marketing.
- I'll fit it to 45 seconds, keep it low under the voice, lift it slightly between lines and on the ending, and fade it out over the last 2 seconds.
