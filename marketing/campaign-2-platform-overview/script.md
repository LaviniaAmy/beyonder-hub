## Script

Times are approximate; I'll fit the animation to the final recordings. **Bold** in the visuals column marks the pop-up card text (one at a time, 3–7 words). Sound effects (SFX) are added by me.

### 0. Opening: the question (0:00 – 0:10)

> **VO 1:** When a child has special educational needs, families need answers. *(pause)* But knowing where to turn… is often the hardest part.

- **Visual:** hero navy sky. A single starling flies in and the Beyonder dot pops (as in Campaign 1). The words "Where do we turn?" fade in, word by word.
- **SFX:** soft wind, a wing flutter, a gentle "pop" on the dot.

### 1. The scale (0:10 – 0:38)

> **VO 2:** Across the UK, more than two million children and young people have special educational needs.
>
> **VO 3:** In England, that's more than one in five pupils.
>
> **VO 4:** Education, Health and Care Plans rose by over twelve percent in a single year, to more than seven hundred thousand.
>
> **VO 5:** And fewer than half are issued within the twenty-week legal time limit.

- **Visual:** numbers count up, big and centred, one per line of VO:
  1. **2,000,000+** with a UK outline that fills with tiny starlings
  2. **1 in 5**, shown as 5 pupil icons with 1 lit in peach
  3. **718,838 EHC plans**, with a bar that grows and a "+12.5% in one year" tag
  4. **46%**, with a 20-week timeline where the bar stops short
- **SFX:** a soft tick as numbers count, and a low "thud" as each lands.

### 2. The problem → Beyonder (0:38 – 0:58)

> **VO 6:** So families search everywhere. *(beat)* WhatsApp groups. *(beat)* Facebook pages. *(beat)* Google. *(beat)* Word of mouth.
>
> **VO 7:** The help is out there, but it's scattered, and hard to trust.
>
> **VO 8:** Beyonder brings it all together, in one place. *(pause)* Everything your family needs, together.

- **Visual:** chat bubbles, search bars and social-style cards pop up all over the screen, getting cluttered. Then a flock of starlings sweeps through and gathers them into one phone, which lands centre screen showing the Beyonder home screen.
- **Pop-up:** **All SEND support. One place.**
- **SFX:** message pings that overlap, getting busier; a whoosh as the starlings sweep in; a calm chime when the phone lands.

### Chapter 1: Parents (0:58 – 1:52)

**Chapter card:** "01 · For parents", with the starling avatar bubble (as in Campaign 1).

> **VO 9:** Here's how it works, starting with parents.
>
> **VO 10:** A parent answers three simple questions: their child's age, what they're finding hard, and what would help most. No diagnosis needed.

- **Visual:** phone shows the question screens. Taps on "5 to 11", then "Speech and communication" and "Sensory", then "Talk it through with a specialist". The screens swipe left between questions.
- **Pop-up:** **3 quick questions. No diagnosis needed.**

> **VO 11:** Beyonder matches them with verified SEND specialists: speech and language therapists, occupational therapists, psychologists and education advisors.
>
> **VO 12:** They can book a live video, phone or chat consultation, often the same day.

- **Visual:** zoom into the results. Scroll down through specialist cards (new names: **Dr Maya Bennett**, educational psychologist; **Tom Ellis**, speech and language therapist; **Priya Shah**, occupational therapist). Tap "Book this time" on Tom's card, then "Booking requested".
- **Pop-ups:** **Verified specialists** → **Book in minutes**

> **VO 13:** Or they can find trusted local support (therapists, clubs, products, schools and charities) on a map of the whole UK.

- **Visual:** swipe to "Find local support". The UK region map: tap the South West, which glows peach. Category chips scroll sideways. Scroll down through local providers (**Brightsteps OT**, **Willow Sensory Club**, **Little Oak Tutoring**).
- **Pop-up:** **Local support, UK-wide**

> **VO 14:** Every enquiry goes straight to the provider, and their child's details are saved securely, so they never have to repeat their story.
>
> **VO 15:** Plus a community of parents, and guides written by experts.

- **Visual:** enquiry form fills in (child's details pre-filled), tap "Send enquiry", then "Enquiry sent ✓". Quick swipe through Profile (child's details saved), Community and Guides.
- **Pop-ups:** **Tell your story once** → **Community + expert guides**
- **SFX (whole chapter):** soft taps, light swipes, a pop for each card, a gentle "sent" swoosh.

### Chapter 2: Providers (1:52 – 2:28)

**Chapter card:** "02 · For providers"

> **VO 16:** Next, providers.
>
> **VO 17:** Therapists, tutors, clubs and SEND professionals create a profile, or claim the listing that's already waiting for them.

- **Visual:** a "Claim your listing" screen. Tap "This is my business", then a verified tick appears on **Brightsteps OT**.
- **Pop-up:** **Claim your listing in minutes**

> **VO 18:** Their dashboard shows new enquiries, bookings, and how families are finding them.

- **Visual:** zoom into the provider dashboard. New enquiry notifications slide in, and the profile-views number counts up.
- **Pop-up:** **Every enquiry in one inbox**

> **VO 19:** They set their availability, their consultation options and their prices, all on their own terms.
>
> **VO 20:** And because consultations happen online, a specialist in Manchester can support a family in Cornwall, reaching families nationwide and earning extra income around their existing work.

- **Visual:** the availability calendar and options (as in Campaign 1, faster). Then zoom out to a UK map with a line arcing from Manchester to Cornwall and a video call bubble.
- **Pop-ups:** **On their own terms** → **Support families nationwide**
- **SFX:** taps, toggle clicks, a soft "ding" for notifications, a whoosh along the map arc.

### Chapter 3: The Beyonder team (2:28 – 2:58)

**Chapter card:** "03 · Behind the scenes"

> **VO 21:** Behind it all is the Beyonder team.
>
> **VO 22:** Every provider is checked and verified before families see them.
>
> **VO 23:** The team monitors quality, invites and onboards new providers, and stays in touch with everyone through built-in messages and announcements.
>
> **VO 24:** So parents can always trust what they find.

- **Visual:** the admin panel on the phone. A list of providers; tap one, then a "Verify" button turns into a green "Verified" badge. Swipe to an invites list with "Invite sent" and "Claimed" statuses ticking over. Then a message composer: "Welcome to Beyonder!" is typed and sent to providers.
- **Pop-ups:** **Every provider verified** → **Quality, monitored** → **Direct messages to every user**
- **SFX:** a stamp-like click on "Verified", a sent swoosh, a soft chime.

### Closing: why it matters (2:58 – 3:25)

> **VO 25:** Beyonder solves real problems for everyone involved.
>
> **VO 26:** Parents find trusted help, faster. *(beat)* Providers reach the families who need them most. *(beat)* And support that was scattered becomes one connected place.
>
> **VO 27:** With need rising every year, there has never been a more important time.
>
> **VO 28:** Beyonder. *(pause)* Everything your family needs, together.
>
> **VO 29:** Find out more at beyonder-hub dot co dot uk.

- **Visual:** back to the hero sky. Three cards rise one after another: **Parents: trusted help, faster** · **Providers: families nationwide** · **Everyone: one connected place**. The starling flies across and the logo forms. Final frame: Beyonder logo, "Everything your family needs, together." and **beyonder-hub.co.uk**.
- **SFX:** the music swells, a final soft chime on the logo, then the music fades out over the last 3 seconds.
